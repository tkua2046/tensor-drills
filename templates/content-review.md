# Content review

Candidate: commit or immutable snapshot identifier.

Review scope: catalog, drill IDs, or changed assessment requirements.

Reviewer and method: identify independent work, inspected materials, executed probes, Python/library versions and sampling limits.

## Coverage

For a catalog review, group drills by primary outcome and overlapping family. Link a larger inventory when useful.

| Track or family | Materials inspected | Probes executed | Limits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ... | ... | ... | ... |

For an assessment review, trace the important requirements to active evidence.

| Requirement | Input or state exercising it | Plausible mistake | Named fixed case or rubric evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ... | ... | ... | ... |

## Findings

Give each finding a stable ID. Distinguish an assessment or discovery defect from a curriculum recommendation or optional improvement. Cite repository-relative file locations, the trigger and consequence, and the evidence supporting the proposed action. Explain why an apparent overlap is justified when keeping separate drills matters.

For a reproduced assessment defect, record the attempt, exact command, expected and observed exit status, and case results. Preserve the reproducer and full output as development evidence. Include seeds, input domains and independent expectations for generated probes. Checked-in summaries should remain understandable without a developer's scratch paths.
